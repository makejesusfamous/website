About MJF

To make the Gospel known in the USA & beyond.

Why the USA

Suicide rates up are up, crime is increasing, homelessness is on the rise, belief in USA declining, morality is in decay, our children are being attacked by adults wanting to increase anti-God agendas. 250 years of beliefs are being destroyed in a short time and it's ALL because Jesus is rarely found in our culture, let alone our churches.

Make Jesus Famous is about spreading the name of Jesus. The task is large and impossible for any individual. We need evangelists who feel the same, so we train up and send out those with a passion to preach Jesus. MJF is committed to training up and helping evangelists reach the lost.

Jesus never said that the harvest was limited, he said the workers are few. MJF does all it can to reached the harvest around the globe because the Gospel is for the world.

Make Jesus Famous conducts international gospel campaigns annually in East Africa, as Dean Morris has a heart for where he first learned how to do festivals.

Where it started

Dean Morris got his start with international stadium-scale ministry with Peter Youngren in Indonesia. Below you will see some highlights from Dean's campaigns in Kurik, Agats, and Dobo.

During Peter Youngren's Friendship Festival in Ziway, Ethiopia, Peter gave Dean his first opportunity to preach in a stadium. Dean says, "Without Peter Youngren and World Impact Ministries, I never would have had the opportunity to preach to the multitudes, let alone know how to make it happen. I will forever be grateful to Pastor Peter and WIM - I owe any success in festival work to him. Without Pastor Peter, there is no Make Jesus Famous Ministries and I would not have the faith to step out and believe Jesus for the impossible."
