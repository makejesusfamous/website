Here is what happens next.

Your request goes to Dean's own prayer board — a real list he works by hand. He prays over it. If it is something you want to talk about, he may reach out himself. And over the next few weeks you will hear real stories from Tanzania, so you can see what your prayers are part of. Nothing automated will ever pretend to be him.

How we pray for you.

We do not pray from a script. Every request is read one name at a time and brought to God the same way we pray over the pastors and villages we serve: by name, believing He hears. We pray for healing. For marriages and for children. For the job, the bill, the diagnosis, the one who walked away from God. For the thing you have not told anyone else. Your request is private. It is never posted, never shared, and never used in anything we publish. If God answers, we would love to hear the story. Scripture says the prayer of a righteous person is powerful and effective (James 5:16). We believe that, and we will stand with you in it.
