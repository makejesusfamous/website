Make Jesus Famous Ministries

Make Jesus Famous is about spreading the name of Jesus. The task is large and impossible for any individual. We need evangelists who feel the same, so we train up and send out those with a passion to preach Jesus. MJF is committed to training up and helping evangelists reach the lost.

Jesus never said that the harvest was limited — He said the workers are few. MJF does all it can to reach the harvest around the globe, because the Gospel is for the world. We conduct international Gospel campaigns annually in East Africa, where Dean Morris first learned how to do festivals. Read more about MJF and Dean →
